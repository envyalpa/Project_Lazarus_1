This meeting was a one-on-one discussion with Bhavya, the Academic Coordinator at Vallath Education, to understand her role, daily responsibilities, pain points, and suggestions for improvement. The session was part of Q4's consulting engagement to streamline operations and implement better systems.

### Discussion Points
- **Background & Experience**: Bhavya has over 13 years of experience as a lab tutor at IIEST (under ISRO), where she handled lab sessions, office work, and even admissions during peak seasons. She joined Vallath Education about two years ago as an academic coordinator.
- **Key Responsibilities**:
  1. **Online Class Scheduling & Link Creation**: Zoom links are created daily (same day for 5 PM classes, previous evening for 7 AM early morning NET classes). Due to Zoom license limitations (4 x 100 participants, 2 x 500 participants), classes may be combined to manage capacity.
  2. **Faculty Briefing**: After each class, Bhavya reviews recordings or contacts the instructor to understand what was covered, then briefs the next instructor to ensure continuity. This is done via phone or WhatsApp voice message.
  3. **Session Initiation & Hosting**: Bhavya or an intern starts the Zoom session, makes the faculty host, enables live streaming, and monitors the session (especially for guest lecturers).
  4. **Student Query Handling**: Students contact faculty via WhatsApp with issues like login problems, missing class notes (PPTs), or technical difficulties. Escalations come from the customer service team.
  5. **Kalyani's Schedule Management**: Bhavya manages Kalyani's meetings and events, often posting weekly schedules on a WhatsApp group (including Sudeep, Anand, Anupama, and Kalyani's husband). Reminders are sent the previous night.
  6. **15-Day Timetable Preparation**: Every 15 days, a timetable is drafted on a whiteboard based on faculty availability (via a facilitators WhatsApp group), then finalized on Google Sheets and shared with students via batch-specific WhatsApp groups.
- **Challenges**:
  - Frequent last-minute faculty unavailability (2-3 instances per week) requiring backups or rescheduling.
  - Kalyani's schedule changes due to her busy calendar; she often misses reminders.
  - Student queries during evening/early morning hours (5 PM–9 PM class window) go unanswered as staff leave at 5:30 PM.
  - Lack of a centralized scheduling system; information is scattered across whiteboard, Google Sheets, and WhatsApp.
  - Difficulty in determining the number of classes needed for each portion (faculty ask "how many classes" but Bhavya lacks subject matter expertise to judge).
- **Suggestions from Bhavya**:
  - Involve faculty in the scheduling process to reduce last-minute cancellations.
  - Provide a strong support person to help with scheduling and student queries.
  - Create a knowledge base/FAQ for common student issues.
- **Next Steps Discussed**:
